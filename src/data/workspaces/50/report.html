National Audit of Pulmonary Hypertension
Local Action Plan — 10th Annual Report

Standards Under Review & Recommended Actions

Standard 1 — PAH Drug Therapy Timeliness

Target

80 % of new referrals who have a diagnosis of idiopathic, heritable, anorexigen-induced or connective tissue disease–associated PAH and are in WHO functional class II, III or IV, should commence PAH drug therapy within 12 weeks of receipt of their first referral.

Issue: PH centres that do not meet this standard need to review delays in the patient pathway.

Recommended action: Increase throughput for diagnostic right heart catheterisation. Met centres expect to meet this standard in the 11th Annual Report.

Standard 2 — Pulmonary Endarterectomy Waiting Time

Target

90 % of patients who undergo a pulmonary endarterectomy should wait less than four months from their diagnosis of chronic thromboembolic pulmonary hypertension (CTEPH).

Issue: NHS England and PH centres must jointly address delays in undertaking pulmonary endarterectomy, as the patient pathway is complex.

Recommended action: Addressed through a joint effort between NHS England and PH centres; see Royal Papworth Hospital response for detail.
