Performance Targets at a Glance

Patient Pathways with Identified Delays

Pathway 1: PAH Therapy Initiation (Page 28)

New referrals with idiopathic, heritable, anorexigen-induced, or connective tissue disease-associated PAH in WHO functional classes II, III, or IV must commence PAH-specific drug therapy within 12 weeks of their first referral. The primary bottleneck is the diagnostic right heart catheterisation step, which currently limits centre throughput. The centre is actively working to increase this capacity and is optimistic about achieving the 80% compliance target by the next report cycle.

Pathway 2: Surgical Intervention for CTEPH (Page 35)

Patients diagnosed with chronic thromboembolic pulmonary hypertension (CTEPH) who are candidates for pulmonary endarterectomy must wait less than four months from diagnosis to surgery. This pathway is more complex as it demands joint coordination between NHS England and the PH centre. The action item references the Royal Papworth Hospital's response, indicating that further detail on remediation steps is contained in a separate document.

Recommended Next Steps

1. Expand Catheterisation Capacity

Prioritise procurement of additional equipment or scheduling time for diagnostic right heart catheterisation procedures to directly address the throughput bottleneck.

2. Formalise Joint Governance

Establish a formal multidisciplinary governance process between NHS England, the local PH centre, and Royal Papworth Hospital to track CTEPH surgical wait times end-to-end.

3. Monitor Month-on-Month Progress

Implement monthly tracking dashboards for both metrics so that deviations from the 12-week and 4-month benchmarks are flagged early, rather than only at annual reporting cycles.

4. Obtain & Analyse Royal Papworth Response

The specific remediation plan from Royal Papworth Hospital needs to be obtained and integrated into the central action tracker to close the CTEPH pathway accountability loop.
